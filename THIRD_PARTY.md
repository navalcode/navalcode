# Media provenance

`images/rumbo.png` and `images/bitacora.png` are unedited captures of the original Rumbo and Bitácora demos running on an Android emulator. Both use fictional data. Rumbo's landscapes are original Canvas illustrations; neither image is a photograph of a physical device or an AI-generated app screenshot.

The demo implementations retain their own licenses and notices: [Rumbo](https://github.com/navalcode/rumbo-android) and [Bitácora](https://github.com/navalcode/bitacora-flutter). Their embedded Outfit/Fraunces typefaces retain SIL Open Font License notices in those projects. This profile introduces no font binary, runtime dependency or replacement project license.
